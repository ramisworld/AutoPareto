## 3. Evidence problems and judgement calls encountered

2. **Draft numbers that did not survive recomputation.**
   - "within 0.004" → **0.0041**.
   - "≤ 0.002" top-5 gaps → **≤ 0.0021**.
   - "SD 0.0021" → **0.0022** (rounded from 0.00215).
