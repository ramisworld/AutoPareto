Abstract
Can the resource contract given to an autonomous researcher change what appears1
to be a scientiﬁc discovery? Fixed-time evaluation entangles model design with2
resource allocation because faster programs process more training data before they3
are ranked. We study this problem with AutoPareto, a code-editing Transformer4
researcher that jointly considers validation quality, cached-decode throughput, and5
allocated inference memory on a single NVIDIA A40. We audit three ﬁxed-time6
discovery campaigns, retrain seven frozen recipes at equal token exposure, and7
begin a prospective ﬁxed-time versus ﬁxed-token budget-contract ablation. The8
discovery-stage validation advantage of the selected AutoPareto recipe does not9
survive equal-token held-out evaluation, showing that the original ranking com-10
bined architecture with unequal exposure. The architecture nevertheless retains a11
substantial deployment advantage: it nearly doubles cached-decoding throughput,12
uses 41.7% fewer parameters and 35.1% less allocated inference memory, and13
halves measured KV -cache memory relative to the selected AutoResearch refer-14
ence. Early contract-pair trajectories pursue different edits, but the current paired15
study is insufﬁcient to identify a causal effect of the contract on researcher behav-16
ior. These results show that resource contracts and exposure accounting belong in17
the speciﬁcation of autonomous ML research.18
1 Part 1: Autonomous research result19
1.1 Introduction20
The resource contract is part of an autonomous research question. Under ﬁxed time, a faster training21
program receives more data before evaluation, so architecture and resource allocation are entangled22
in the score. Under ﬁxed tokens, exposure is controlled, but wall time, update count, and compute23
may vary. A discovered improvement can therefore reﬂect the contract as much as the program edit.24
This problem becomes especially visible when an agent is allowed to change architecture, batching,25
and optimization together.26
We study this problem in small language-model training on one NVIDIA A40. AutoPareto gives27
a coding agent three explicit objectives: validation BPB, cached-decode throughput, and allocated28
inference memory. The agent edits the training program rather than selecting only from a ﬁxed29
vector. We audit its archive against a quality-only AutoResearch campaign and ﬁxed-vector ran-30
dom search, TPE, MOTPE, NSGA-II, and CMA-ES. The classical comparison asks whether source-31
editing search uncovers useful regions that ordinary optimizers miss. Unequal spaces and historical32
budgets make it diagnostic rather than a controlled tournament.33
Our central result separates discovery from veriﬁcation. AutoPareto’s best archived validation score34
does not survive equal-token held-out evaluation, which shows that the discovery ranking mixed35

1.2 Discovery protocol and comparators52
The baseline is an eight-layer, 512-wide decoder-only Transformer with 50.33M parameters, trained53
on ClimbMix-400B-shufﬂe with a project-speciﬁc 8,192-token vocabulary and 2,048-token packed54
sequences. Historical AutoPareto comprises three 25-attempt campaigns. Each attempt gives the55
agent a parent program and prior results, then permits a source-code edit before a ﬁve-minute A4056
run. Protected evaluators measure validation BPB, cached decoding, and memory. The quality-only57
AutoResearch archive uses the same model family and task, but its recovered campaigns differ in58
prompt text, commits, wrappers, and feedback. We therefore do not claim that the objective string59
was the only operational difference.60
Table 1 reports every recovered comparison family. AutoPareto attains the lowest archived validation61
BPB, 1.0933, followed by AutoResearch at 1.0950 and TPE at 1.0977. Selection on these values can62
be optimistic, so the paper’s primary comparison uses unseen conﬁrmation seeds and held-out BPB.63
Ferreira et al. similarly show that classical HPO remains a strong baseline for autoresearch-style64
problems and that code-editing and ﬁxed-vector search expose different capabilities [ Ferreira et al. ,65
2026].66

2.1 Codex researcher and trusted harness123
Humans deﬁne the task, objective contract, editable surface, budget, hardware, and acceptance rules.124
Codex CLI runs gpt-5.6-sol at high reasoning with a 600-second scientist timeout. Each invocation125
receives ﬁve staged ﬁles in an ephemeral, read-only container; only train.py is writable. The two126
budget arms have separate histories and are interleaved on the same A40. Initial executable bytes127
match across arms.128
The trusted controller, which the researcher cannot edit, validates source and parent hashes, sends129
the candidate to the A40 worker, runs training, checks the checkpoint, performs protected BPB130
and deployment evaluation, records logs and lineage, then constructs the next campaign view. A131
candidate may fail technically, complete with poor quality, or remain speed-uncertain. Those states132
are preserved and are not interchangeable.133

2.5 Signiﬁcance, related work, and limits184
The useful ﬁnding is not that reducing depth increases speed. It is that a deployment-aware code-185
editing researcher explored a broader architectural lineage and produced multiple efﬁciency-quality186
points that survived equal-token retraining on a single A40. The measured quality cost prevents a187
stronger claim. The lineage and failed branches show which hypotheses led to that point and which188
simple reductions did not work.189
A recovered historical component ablation around experiment 68 provides a separate caution. Three190
batch-only runs average validation BPB 1.1040 at 88.1M tokens, while three nonbatch-only runs191
average 1.2188 at 94.4M tokens. The contrast supports batch size as an important discovery-stage192
mechanism, but its unequal exposures prevent a clean causal decomposition. We therefore keep193
this component ablation separate from the prospective budget-contract ablation and the equal-token194
conﬁrmation panel.195
Primer searches low-level Transformer primitives under an implicit efﬁciency objective [ So et al. ,196
2021]. einspace builds networks from a grammar of fundamental operations [ Ericsson et al. , 2024];197
MOTE-NAS combines landscape and convergence estimates [ Zhang et al., 2024]; MONAQ queries198
LLMs for resource-aware architectures [ Trirat and Lee , 2025]. Hyperband and BOHB allocate199
resources across conﬁgurations [ Li et al. , 2018, Falkner et al. , 2018]. AutoDiscovery, MLE-STAR,200
AI-Researcher, and MLE-Dojo address hypothesis search, targeted reﬁnement, broader research201
automation, and interactive ML engineering environments [ Agarwal et al. , 2025, Nam et al. , 2025,202
Tang et al., 2025, Qiang et al. , 2025]. AutoPareto’s narrower contribution is an auditable, objective-203
conditioned source-editing loop with matched-exposure veriﬁcation.204
The study has one hardware family and small models. Conﬁrmation seeds estimate recipe variability,205
not independent search-policy variability. Historical comparators have unequal spaces, seeds, and206
failure histories. The prospective budget comparison is one incomplete adaptive trajectory per arm,207
with repaired protocols and no valid deployment frontier. BLiMP analysis is aggregate. Proprietary208
agent behavior and service conﬁguration limit exact reproduction. We make no scale-transfer, cross-209
GPU, causal budget-effect, energy-saving, or universal optimizer-superiority claim.210

3 Part 3: Broader impact and reﬂections211
Resource contracts are part of the scientiﬁc speciﬁcation of autonomous research. When an agent212
controls architecture and training, a time allowance changes how much data a faster candidate sees.213
A token allowance removes that exposure pathway but permits update count and compute to vary.214
Papers should therefore publish the stopping rule, schedule-progress variable, warm-up boundary,215
inference workload, and feedback returned after each attempt.216
Complete trajectories should retain crashes, unstable measurements, and scientiﬁcally poor but tech-217
nically completed runs. Parent eligibility also matters. Allowing a quality-valid, speed-uncertain218
candidate to seed a later experiment can keep a campaign moving through timing noise, but it219
changes the search policy and must be recorded.220
Efﬁciency may reduce the cost of an individual experiment or deployment, yet cheaper experiments221
can increase total experimentation. We report no energy beneﬁt because the artifacts do not contain222
a campaign-wide energy accounting. Small-model benchmark results also do not establish safe or223
reliable use in consequential settings.224
Human accountability grows with experiment volume. Humans set objectives, repair infrastructure,225
decide which failures are scientiﬁc, and interpret evidence. An auditable record should distinguish226
the human question, the agent’s prediction, the source edit, the measured outcome, and the retro-227
spective explanation. The pipeline accompanying this paper rebuilds every table and vector ﬁgure228
from frozen artifacts and reports changes to headline values without launching experiments.229
